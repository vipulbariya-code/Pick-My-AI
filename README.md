## 📖 About

**Pick My AI** is a modern platform that helps users discover and explore the best AI tools for productivity, coding, design, writing, marketing, education, and more.

Whether you're a developer, student, creator, or business owner, Pick My AI makes it easy to find the right AI tool for your needs.
